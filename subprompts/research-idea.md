# Research Idea

Read and follow `ai-framework/01-research-idea.md`.

## Web search requirement

During Stage 2 (Market & Competitor Scan), **use web search** to find real competitors, pricing, recent funding, and market data. Do not rely on training data alone -- it may be outdated or incomplete. Search for:

- Direct competitors and their current feature sets
- Recent market reports or industry trends
- Pricing models and monetization strategies
- User reviews or complaints about existing solutions (Reddit, G2, Product Hunt)

Cite sources when presenting competitor data.
